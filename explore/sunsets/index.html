Chasing the last light

Five sunsets I still remember, from the water’s edge to the top of a mountain.

By Albert Ching · November 25, 2026

Ever since I was little, I have been chasing sunsets. I grew up in Waikīkī, where I watched the sun drop toward the western horizon over the water. I learned to look for a few clouds: on the best evenings, they held the color long after the sun had gone.

These are five sunsets that have stayed with me. Some were softened by a tropical sky, others by the light of autumn. I can still remember where I stood for each one.

01 / Thailand

Railay Bay

Longtail boats at rest, limestone cliffs in silhouette, and a sky that kept getting warmer as the light disappeared.

02 / Indonesia

Seminyak, Bali

At the edge of the beach, with palms and people gathering for the last color of the day.

03 / California

The Golden Gate Bridge

One of my favorite San Francisco views: the sun finding the bridge just as the bay turns dark.

04 / Greece

Kalymnos

A quiet moment at the water’s edge, when the sun seemed to rest on the horizon.

05 / Hawaiʻi

Mauna Kea

Above the clouds on the Big Island, the scale of the sky makes the final moments of daylight feel different.
