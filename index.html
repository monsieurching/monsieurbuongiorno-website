A note from Monsieur

Why I made this little corner of the internet.

So many of the recommendation sites I come across are written by women. I wanted to add my own perspective: I’m a straight, high-feeling dad who loves the perfect little things. Some are luxurious. Many are free.

I grew up in Waikīkī, and now I’m a dad and perfumer in San Francisco, making Maison Buongiorno perfumes by hand. I needed a place to share the recommendations I’m uniquely passionate about—the places, scents, objects, and everyday rituals I keep coming back to.

This is that place. A personal collection of things I love enough to tell a friend about.

Monsieur
