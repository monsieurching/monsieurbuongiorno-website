The bottles I open when there’s a reason to pause

A first glass of Champagne in Boston, a wedding Champagne hour, and the half bottle I save for the best occasions.

By Albert Ching · Founder and perfumer, Maison Buongiorno

My love affair with Champagne began when I was a young management consultant in Boston. Finale, a buzzing dessert stop in Back Bay, was where I discovered Veuve Clicquot. After the cheap rum and vodka of college, those first glasses felt like an awakening. I remember how bright and alert I felt, as if my senses had turned up.

Since then, I’ve kept returning to the big houses. I know there are extraordinary grower Champagnes to explore, but these bottles are attached to places, people, and moments in my life. That’s part of their appeal.

The special occasion · Krug

Grande Cuvée, half bottle

For the very best occasions, I reach for a half bottle of Krug Grande Cuvée. I love its silky texture and the depth that comes from blending wines across many years. If a friend opens Dom Pérignon or Cristal, I certainly won’t refuse a glass. But this is the bottle I’d choose for myself.

After Champagne: Calvados

Champagne is always an invitation to gather, pause, and celebrate. When the air turns colder and the evening slows down, I also love a Calvados over ice, especially one that brings both apple and pear into the glass.

A colder weather glass · Lemorton

Sélection Calvados Domfrontais

Lemorton is the one I have in mind. The younger Sélection has the pear and apple character I love, and it is lovely on the rocks.
