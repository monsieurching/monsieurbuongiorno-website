Rituals · Notes from my everyday

My favorite daily rituals

Small moments I want to make room for, from the first light of morning to the last page before sleep.

By Monsieur Buongiorno

A little space to begin

Wake up without a phone

Let the day begin before the screen.

A cold shower

For those deep breaths.

Move, breathe, come back

Swim or work out with others

A swim at the YMCA, or a hard workout with others at Barry’s.

Be there for the everyday

Walk with Charlie and Pepita

Without a phone.

Pick up or drop off Mossi

Make room for this part of the day.

Bedtime or a shower with Mossi

Time together at the end of the day.

Let the day go

Read a magazine or a book

A few pages to settle into.

Sleep with gratitude and surrender

Be grateful. Let go.

Imagined scenes, illustrated for the Journal of Monsieur Buongiorno.
