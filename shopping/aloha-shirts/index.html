The wardrobe / Hawaiʻi / Five favorites

My favorite men’s aloha shirts

The best aloha shirts, for me, are usually made of cotton—and bring out the chill vibes in others. These are the five brands I keep coming back to.

By Monsieur Buongiorno
01 / Honolulu

Salvage Public

For a modern take

For me, these are the best modern takes on aloha shirts: Hawaiian-centric, with the easy surfing spirit that runs through everything Salvage Public does.

Shop Salvage Public ↗
02 / Hilo

Sig Zane

For the originals

The OG. A little looser in fit, with the graphic excellence of Uncle Sig and the sacred botanicals that are part of hula culture. Shirts from Hilo that I love for what they honor as much as how they look.

Shop Sig Zane ↗
03 / Chinatown, Honolulu

Roberta Oaks

For a slimmer cut

My first stop for a slim-cut aloha shirt. I keep returning to the unique, retro, playful patterns—especially the paisleys.

Shop Roberta Oaks ↗
04 / Multiple locations

Tori Richard

For a special occasion

When I want an elegant shirt for a special occasion, like a wedding, this is where I often turn. Sometimes I want something other than cotton—a fabric like silk that feels a little more dressed up.

Shop Tori Richard ↗
05 / Multiple locations

Kahala

For the long run

My high school class shirt was Kahala. It ages so well: the kind of shirt that feels as though it could last a lifetime.

Shop Kahala ↗
